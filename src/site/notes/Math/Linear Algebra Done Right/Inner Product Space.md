# Inner Product Space
>[!example] Definition of inner product space
>An inner product space is a [[Math/Linear Algebra Done Right/Vector Space\|vector space]] $V$ along with an [[Math/Linear Algebra Done Right/Inner Product\|inner product]] on $V$.
