# Dot Product
>[!example] Definition of dot product
>For $x,y\in$[[Math/Linear Algebra Done Right/Dimensional Spaces\|$\mathbf{R}^n$]], the dot product of $x$ and $y$, denoted $x\cdot y$, is defined by 
> $$
> x\cdot y=x_{1}y_{1}+\dots+x_{n}y_{n}
> $$ 
> where $x=(x_{1},\dots,x_{n})$ and $y=(y_{1},\dots y_{n})$
