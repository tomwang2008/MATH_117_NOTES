# Dual Basis

>[!example] Dual Basis
>If $v_{1},\dots,v_{n}$ is a [[Math/Linear Algebra Done Right/Basis\|basis]] of [[Math/Linear Algebra Done Right/Vector Space\|$V$]], then the dual basis of $v_{1},\dots,v_{n}$ is the list $\varphi_{1},\dots,\varphi_{n}$ of elements of $V'$. The definition of $\varphi_{j}$ is:
> $$
> \varphi_{j}(v_{k})=
> \begin{cases}
>1 &\text{if }i=j\\
>0 &\text{if }i\neq j
>\end{cases}
> $$

Just as shown above, the dual basis is a basis for [[Math/Linear Algebra Done Right/Dual Basis\|dual space]].
