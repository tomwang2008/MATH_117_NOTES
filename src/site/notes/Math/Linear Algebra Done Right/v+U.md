# $v+U$

>[!example] Definition $v+U$
>Suppose $v\in V$ and $U$ is a [[Math/Linear Algebra Done Right/Subspace\|subspace]] of [[Math/Linear Algebra Done Right/Vector Space\|$V$]]. Then $v+U$ is the subset of $V$ defined by
> $$
> v+U=\{ v+u\ :\ u\in U\}.
> $$
