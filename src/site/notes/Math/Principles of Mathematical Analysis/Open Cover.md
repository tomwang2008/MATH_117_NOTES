# Open Cover
> [!example] Definition of open cover
> By an open cover of a [[Math/Principles of Mathematical Analysis/Set\|set]] $E$ in a [[Math/Principles of Mathematical Analysis/Metric Space\|metric space]] $X$ we mean a collection $\{ G_{\alpha} \}$ of [[Math/Principles of Mathematical Analysis/Open Set\|open]] subsets of $X$ such that $E\subset \bigcup_{\alpha}G_{\alpha}$.
