# Dense
> [!example] Definition of dense 
> $E$ is dense in [[Math/Principles of Mathematical Analysis/Metric Space\|$X$]] if every point of $X$ is a [[Math/Principles of Mathematical Analysis/Limit Point\|limit point]] of $E$, or a point of $E$
